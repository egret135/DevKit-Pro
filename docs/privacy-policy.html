English

Overview

DevKit Pro is an offline-first developer toolkit that runs entirely in your browser. It does not operate a backend server for processing your content, and it does not sell or share your data with advertisers.

Data we store locally

The extension may store the following on your device only:

DataPurposeStorage
Editor preferences (theme, font, indent)Remember UI settingschrome.storage / localStorage
Generation options (package name, TableName)Remember converter settingschrome.storage / localStorage
Draft Markdown textRestore unfinished inputlocalStorage

You can clear this data by clearing the extension site data, uninstalling the extension, or using in-app clear actions.

Data we do not collect

  • We do not upload your DDL, JSON, Markdown, images, audio, video, passwords, tokens, or other tool inputs to our servers.
  • We do not use analytics SDKs, advertising trackers, or third-party tracking pixels.
  • We do not require an account.

Formatting, conversion, preview, export, and hashing happen locally in the browser.

Permissions

PermissionWhy
storageSave preferences and options on your device
downloadsSave exported files when you choose to download

Copy uses the browser clipboard API only when you click Copy. The extension does not request the clipboardWrite permission. It does not read browsing history or arbitrary websites.

Third-party libraries

Bundled libraries (CodeMirror, Marked, Mermaid, KaTeX, MediaInfo WASM, and others) run locally inside the extension and are not used to send your content elsewhere.

Children

DevKit Pro is intended for developers and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy when features or permissions change. The date above will be revised. Continued use means you accept the updated policy.

概述

DevKit Pro 是一款在浏览器本地运行的开发者工具扩展。我们没有用于处理你内容的后台服务器，也不会向广告商出售或共享你的数据。

本地存储

数据用途位置
编辑器偏好记住界面设置chrome.storage / localStorage
生成选项记住转换器配置chrome.storage / localStorage
Markdown 草稿恢复未完成输入localStorage

我们不收集的内容

  • 不会上传 DDL、JSON、Markdown、图片、音视频、密码或 Token。
  • 不使用分析或广告追踪。
  • 不需要注册账号。

权限

权限用途
storage在本地保存偏好与选项
downloads在你选择导出时保存文件到本机

复制仅在你点击「复制」时通过浏览器剪贴板完成。扩展不申请 clipboardWrite，也不读取浏览历史或任意网站。

第三方库

打包的开源库均在扩展内本地运行，不会回传你的内容。

儿童与变更
